## Don / Decision‑Making Notes (pp.117, 121–123)

### Decision‑Making Muscle (p.117)

Key thoughts on building people who can scale an organization: [file:1]

- High‑quality team‑based decisions sit at the core of scalable organizations.
- The “Seats” require lateral management and shared responsibility.
- If only one person can make many decisions, the right team is not in place. [file:1]

Basic decision process:

1. Data
2. Debate / discussion
3. Decide
4. Defer (when appropriate). [file:1]

### Visionary vs. Operator vs. Process (p.117)

- Too much visionary: “Visionary arsonist.”
- Too much operator: maverick.
- Too much process: bureaucrat.
- Too much synergy: pressure to please people. [file:1]

Goal: institutionalize the leader’s vision so it outlives the person. [file:1]

### Capitals Framework (p.125–126)

Five capitals and their “currencies”: [file:1]

1. Spiritual – God’s presence, wisdom, and power; currency is obedience and intimacy.
2. Relational – family and friends; currency is trust and time.
3. Physical – time and health; currency is energy and strength.
4. Intellectual – creativity and knowledge; currency is ideas and concepts.
5. Financial – money and assets; currency is dollars, products, and contracts. [file:1]

Investment: disciplined, repeatable process of reallocating all five capitals toward Kingdom goals. [file:1]

Scripture anchor: Revelation 21:3–4 picture of abundance across all five capitals. [file:1]
