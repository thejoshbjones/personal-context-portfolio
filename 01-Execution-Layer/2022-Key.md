## Symbols

- `>` – Task.
- `X` – Completed task.
- `O` – Event.
- `-` – Note.
- `*` – Important.
- `>>` – Short‑term tasks worth the time to migrate.
- `<` – Long‑term tasks that are not worth the time.
- `~` – Crossed‑out tasks that are not worth the time.
- `!` – Inspiration, paired with a note (great ideas, mantras, insights).

## Threading

- `>-MM-DD` or `#-MM-DD` – Thread forward to another date/page.
- `<p#-MM-DD` – Thread backward to a previous page/date.
- In‑line arrows like `<-- 2021‑12‑13 → pg 32-April` indicate where an item came from and where it continues.

## Delegation

- `@Name` – Delegated task (e.g. `@kevin - this action`).
- Once the delegate completes it, mark the line with `X`.

## Action key

Structure: `[Action][Person][Subject][Shortcode]`.

Actions:

- `Email` – Send an email.
- `Call` – Make a phone call.
- `Sch` – Schedule.
- `F.U.` – Follow‑up.

Shortcodes:

- `Q` – Question.
- `Obs` – Observation.
- `Sta` – Statement.
- `Opin` – Opinion.
- `Tht` – Thought.

Use combinations like:

- `Email @Scott – SOC project Q`
- `Call @Lisa – payroll Sta`
- `F.U. @Don – Baton Rouge trip Obs`
