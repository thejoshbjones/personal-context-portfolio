"2 concepts to keep for future endeavors if you have to do any analysis."
""
"1) Data Jujitsu -> When there is a massive problem that are ways to consistently break down any problem into it's smaller components. By solving each you are able to use the "weight" (compounding effect of those smaller solutions) of the overall problem against itself for a final solution."
"2) Lossy Compression and Expansion -> The reason AI is better at internet search results is because it uses a version of a lossy compression. Taking the whole of a large dataset and reducing it down into only the parts that you need."
""
"So that's the solve here: Compress the data down to it's smallest most unique parts to develop a "key" to translate everything. Then apply the key and compound back out to the larger dataset."
""
"E.G. 35463 line items becomes 4672 unique items that then reduce down again into 388 types of products that match up to the list of 306 actual products."
""
"306 = 4672. This is your key. "
"Apply the key back out to all 35463 that touch all 3509 clients."
