I had a marathon of annual reviews this morning. These used to be annoying. "Every year, do this thing HR needs you to do." Then I grew. I began to see it differently and now it's a favorite of mine. "
""
"Time and business usually move to fast and we aren't always in the habit of taking a breath to stop, look around, and celebrate how far we've gone. This ends up being a great opportunity for that kind of "Selah" moment."
""
"Managing well involves giving  both the challenging and the encouraging feedback more readily as you work with someone. If you're doing that, then an annual review is simply a way to step back and celebrate the year with an employee. You're both already on the same page, so none of that is a surprise."
""
"-Don't withhold encouraging or challenging feedback."
"-Present feedback readily and consistently."
"-Let the annual review be an opportunity for celebration of the work accomplished."
