I had a discussion last week with some amazing leaders of some amazing Technology Providers. One of the things we discussed was Best in Class metrics."
""
"It was interesting that we all asked similar questions about service design. What became apparent is that the goal wasn't to match up metrics, but to have an excellent experience."
""
"I've referenced Goodhart's law before. "When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure." Another well worn thought is Charlie Munger's idea of "Show me the incentive, and I'll show you the outcome.""
""
"These two ideas play together well."
"Data is only useful if it drives decisions and decisions are only useful if they drive the intended outcomes."
""
"There are many times where industry standard, best in class, metrics do not make sense."
""
"No one cares about how many "single technician closure" tickets exist if it takes 3 weeks to fix a problem or if the the problem keeps showing up."
""
"A better path is to start with the end experience in mind and work backwards using best in class metrics as necessary, alongside service and process design, to create the incentives that drive the outcomes you want to see.
