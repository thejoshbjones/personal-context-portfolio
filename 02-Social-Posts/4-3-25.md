I used to talk about scalability a lot. Which was funny considering the size of the business I was in. "
""
"As the org size has changed I've noticed this thing that happens in my head. "
"Someone talks about a process uses the word scalable. I listen and think "that doesn't work at double the size". "
""
"I don't normally share this because it would fall on deaf ears. I know because it did for me too. I was the deaf ears. You don't realize how many exceptions you're making at the size you are until you outgrow the utility of those exceptions at a larger size."
""
"It's not that unscalable is bad and it's not that the process designs are poor at a smaller size. They're just intended for the size you're currently at and not the size you will be."
""
"There's a consistency that at multiples of 3 and 10 you have to overhaul the organizational processes. "
"Think about it with a family"
"-start with 2 kids"
"-go to 3 kids --> change the car out to accomodate"
"-go to 10 kids --> change the car out to accomodate"
""
"It's not that you need to overhaul everything for this today. Changing processes causes organizational whiplash and there are times that is not worth it until necessary. However, if you intend to grow, ask yourself "what can you do today that positions you better at those multiples so that you don't have as much whiplash in a few months or years?"
