Are you earning money or making money?"
""
"There are two types of thoughts around the transaction of time/energy for capital."
"1) Earning Money: An equal trade off"
"       -e.g. I give this amount and you give me this amount"
"       -Every conversation about greater responsibility requires some returned"
"         value back."
"        -boundaries are extremely clear to the person offering the labor"
"2) Making Money: An agreement about value creation"
"       -e.g. I help you solve this problem. It creates more value for you, but you're"
"         also giving me, in compensation terms, more value than the activity would"
"         give me by executing the same activity on my own."
""
"Earning money is about a paycheck and boundaries."
"Making money is about creating value for yourself and those around you."
""
"I'm not saying one is worse than the other. They each have their own tradeoffs. Each has their own strength. I do have my own preference though. If you find yourself seeing a conversation regarding your pay as a 0 sum game and you have a limited upside potential, you might want to rethink the perspective and the approach.
