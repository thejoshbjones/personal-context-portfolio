There was one point in life that I read about some features on Windows Servers for synchronizing files across locations. It was a feature called DFS (Distributed File System). "
""
"Now, years later three key things stick out:"
"1) Do not use this with Databases"
"2) It is location aware"
"3) Last One Out Wins"
""
"The issue is that I wasn't clear of the impact of those. I set this up for my company between the two main sites on a folder structure that contained a database type system. The next day a coworker called me because all her work was missing. Poor lady was halfway into retirement and had all teh anxiety because she thought she had done something wrong."
""
"Turns out it was the system I had built. "
""
"After further investigation, I realized what had happened. She was working on a site with the folders, while someone else was working from another site with those same folder. She left earlier than the other person, so her work was overwritten."
""
"If they had been working off the same file, then there would have been no issue and I wouldn't have caused a senior citizen to have more stress than is warranted in a day. From that point on I have known what those three concepts mean and the impact they carry."
