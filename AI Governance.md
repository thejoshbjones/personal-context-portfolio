##Guiding Principles

Individual Guiding Principles
1) Responsibility and Accountability
	1) The human is ultimately responsible. (Your digital robot's work is ultimately your work.)
	2) Value: Own It

2) Trust
	1) Trust is a foundation that is created and supported by privacy and security. (Don't misuse data.)
	2) Value: Do the Right Thing, Focus on Others

3) Learning and Curiosity
	1) Continuous Improvement and Ideation driven by curiosity is fundamental. (Like all instruments, you have to play with it to learn how to use it.)
	2) Value: Work Smart

4) Smart Actions
	1) Time to capacity tradeoff is a necessary decision matrix. (Don't be afraid to "spend money" and get back time.)
	2) Value: Work Smart

5) Resource Stewardship
	1) Tokens and Credits are best used when agentic function is required or helpful. (There are times that the user experience will move you away from AI. That is also ok.)
	2) Value: Work Smart, Focus on Others
