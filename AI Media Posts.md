"We tend to overestimate the effect of a technology in the short run and underestimate the effect in the long run." -Amara's Law, 1978

3 things
curiousity

humanity

agility

## Core Principles of Prompt Engineering

Natural Language, Specificity, Provide Context, Clear Objectives, Output Formatting, One Request at a Time (think in a step by step way), Precise Verbs (analyze, summarize, compare, list, explain), Define Parameters, Iterative Refinement (it's a conversation more than a command), Examples
