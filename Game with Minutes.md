## Spec
- I need a app that works primarily on apple watch and syncs to a localized database on the iPhone.
- I want the watch app to be a reminder app that can vibrate your wrist ever in an interval that the user selects. 
- Information about the intervals:
	- The user has an option to turn the reminder on or off, but when on can only select one interval at a time.
	- Those intervals are: 1 minute, 5 minutes, 10 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 4 hours, 6 hours and 8 hours.
	- The user will also need the ability to set a daily range of time in which the alerts start and end.
- Information about the selection features:
	- When the reminder hits they have 30 seconds to acknowledge the alert. Once they acknowledge the alert, the vibrations will stop. 
	- The user should have an option to disable the alerting entirely
	- The user should also be allowed to adjust the alert frequency when needed
- Tie back to iPhone
	- This should be trackable so the user can see their acknowledgement on a daily, weekly, monthly, and annual basis.
	- The way to track the acknowledgement shouldn't be granular beyond a % of the frequencies above.
		- This setup should allow for less data storage usage since the minute by minute or hour by hour acknowledgements can be truncated to a total daily % that can then be used to aggregate into the weekly, monthly and annual basis.
	- There also should be an option on the iPhone to not only see the daily % but to see a trending chart of this.
	- All of the data will be referred to as awareness of presence.
- Specific to the iPhone
	- There should be an ability to adjust the app in the future to include a daily quote based on data that will be entered in a future update of the app.

You win your minute if during that minute you either

1. Pray
2. Recall God.
3. Sing or hum a devotional hymn.
4. Talk or write about God.
5. Seek to relieve suffering of any kind in a prayerful spirit.
6. Work with the consciousness of God’s presence.
7. Whisper to God.
8. Feel yourself encompassed by God.
9. Look at a picture or a symbol of Christ.
10. Read a scripture verse or poem about God.
11. Give somebody a helpful hand for the Lord’s sake.
12. Breathe a prayer for the people you meet.
13. Follow the leading of the Inner Voice.
14. Plan or work for the Kingdom of God.
15. Testify to others about God, the church, or this game.
16. Share suffering or sorrow with another.
17. Hear God and see Him in flowers, trees, water, hills, sky.

We never attempt to keep a minute-by-minute record (except perhaps occasionally for an hour), since such a record would interfere with a normal life. We are practicing a new freedom, not a new bondage. We must not get so tied down to score keeping that we lose the glory of it, and its spontaneity. We fix our eyes upon Jesus, not upon a clock.
