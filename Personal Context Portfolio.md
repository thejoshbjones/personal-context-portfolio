## Principles
1) Markdown-first
2) Modular, not monolithic
3) Living, not static
4) Portable across everything

## File Structure
1) [[Identity.md]]
	1) Who you are in one page - the file an agent reads if it can only read one
2) [[role-and-responsibilities.md]]
	1) What your weeks actually look like, not what your job description says
3) [[current-projects.md]]
	1) Active workstreams, status, priority, what done looks like
4) [[team-and-relationships.md]]
	1) key people, how you interact, what they need from you
5) [[tools-and-systems.md]]
	1) Your stack, your setup, what connects to what
6) [[communication-style.md]]
	1) How you write, How you want things written for you
7) [[goals-and-priorities.md]]
	1) What you're optimizing for and what you're deliberately ignoring
8) [[preferences-and-constraints.md]]
	1) Hard rules and strong opinions any agent should respect
9) [[domain-knowledge.md]]
	1) What you know what a general-purpose AI doestn'
10) [[decision-log.md]]
	1) How you make decisions, with real examples
11) ai-agent-instructions.md
	1) the "What You Must Ask Me Before Doing" rules

## The Process
1) AI Interviews you
	1) Interview -> Draft -> Reaction -> Revision -> Done. Same loop for each file.
