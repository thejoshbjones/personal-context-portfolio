# Current Projects

## Legacy Datacenters Shutdown

- **One-line description:** Turn off all datacenters that are not part of the IT Voice long-term plan, consolidating workloads into the target footprint.
- **Current status:** In progress; three legacy datacenters were in scope, two have already been shut down, and the final site is expected to be ready to turn off by the end of this quarter.
- **Your role:** Direct owner of the initiative, responsible for sequencing, risk management, and communication with leadership and operations stakeholders.
- **Key collaborators:** Datacenter and infrastructure leads, including Dub Carter and related operations teams.
- **What done looks like:** The remaining datacenter is fully migrated, validated, and ready to be powered down safely from an application, data, and risk perspective, leaving only the IT Voice long-term datacenter footprint in operation.
- **Current priority:** High, but bounded; the main focus is executing the final shutdown cleanly and on schedule this quarter.
