# Domain Knowledge

## Managed Services Operations, Operational Maturity, and ITIL

- **Depth:** Deep operator.
- **What this means:** Has designed managed services operations multiple times and knows how to implement operational maturity and ITIL-aligned service standardization in real organizations.
- **Key concepts and terms:**
  - **OML (Operational Maturity Level):** A Service Leadership framework for measuring how consistently and intentionally a technology service provider operates, based on Service Leadership's SLIQ.
  - **ITIL (Information Technology Infrastructure Library):** An industry-standard framework and best-practices library for aligning IT services with business needs.
- **Expectations for agents:** Do not explain OML or ITIL at a beginner level. Assume comfort with maturity models, service lifecycle concepts, and the practical realities of running managed services operations.

## Service Leadership, Standard Chart of Accounts, and SLIQ

- **Depth:** Strong working knowledge.
- **What this means:** Has worked in multiple businesses using the Service Leadership model and SLIQ, knows the concepts and can discuss them fluently, but has not personally led a full migration of a company onto the model.
- **Key concepts and terms:**
  - **Service Leadership Standard Chart of Accounts:** A standardized financial structure for technology service providers to normalize revenue, cost, and margin reporting across services and business models.
  - **SLIQ (Service Leadership Intelligent Quotient):** Service Leadership's tool for measuring primary business model, performance, and profit of a technology service provider.
- **Expectations for agents:** It is safe to reference or build on SLIQ and Service Leadership concepts with minimal explanation. If suggesting changes to financial structure, proposals should respect how standard technology services charts of accounts work.

## Executive Decision Framing and Expected Value

- **Depth:** Deep operator.
- **What this means:** Frames executive decisions and tradeoffs regularly and presents data on them weekly, including expected-value-style thinking.
- **Key concepts and terms:**
  - **EV (Expected Value Calculation):** The anticipated average outcome of an event or investment over the long term, calculated by multiplying each possible outcome by its probability and summing the results. In betting or investing, a positive EV scenario is mathematically favorable over time.
- **Expectations for agents:** Decision support should be structured around options, tradeoffs, and EV-style reasoning where appropriate, not purely qualitative. It is safe to use EV terminology and probability-weighted thinking without basic definitions.

## Integrations, Rollups, and Owner Acquisition

- **Depth:** Deep operator.
- **What this means:** Has spent years working on integrations in a rollup/platform model and understands owner acquisition processes in practice, not just in theory.
- **Key concepts and terms:**
  - **Rollup / Platform model:** The process of acquiring multiple companies in different markets to create one platform that is more powerful together than the individual parts.
  - **Owner Acquisition Process:** The process an owner must go through in selling a business wherein they are able to healthily disconnect themselves from the business so that the business and the owner can both still be valuable in creating outcomes for the acquired and acquiring organizations.
- **Expectations for agents:** Treat this as a primary domain. Integration plans, communication to sellers, and platform narratives should assume a nuanced understanding of both the operational and human sides of acquisitions.
