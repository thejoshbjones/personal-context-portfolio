# Team and Relationships

## Will Slappey (CEO)

- **Relationship to me:** One of my two direct bosses and a primary executive stakeholder for integrations, infrastructure, and long-term platform decisions.
- **How we typically interact:** Weekly 1:1s, multiple collaboration and update meetings, plus email and Microsoft Teams communication.
- **What he needs from me:** Visibility into what is happening, clear decision-making input, and a practical understanding of the tradeoffs behind major decisions.
- **What I need from him:** Strategic direction, executive alignment, and final decisions on major tradeoffs involving capital, risk, and organizational impact.
- **Relevant context:** He prefers someone to come with an intended decision and the reasoning behind it, not just a status update. Tradeoffs matter.
